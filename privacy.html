Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the website of Sarath English Medium School (also known as Sarath Public School).

Information we collect

This website has no login and no database. It does not store personal data on any server.

The admission enquiry form

The enquiry form does not send anything by itself. It only prepares a WhatsApp message, which you can choose to send to the school on +91 94413 68036.

Anything you share with us on WhatsApp, by phone or by email is used only to respond to your enquiry. It is not sold or shared.

Cookies, fonts and hosting

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Fonts load from Google Fonts. The website is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and browser) for security.

Children's privacy

Photos and videos of students are published by the school for school purposes.

Parents can ask for any photo or video of their child to be removed by emailing sarathschool2001@gmail.com. The school will remove it promptly.

Other services

Links to Instagram, WhatsApp and Google Maps follow those services' own privacy policies.
