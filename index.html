Our Legacy

Pedagantyada's premier school for skill-based learning

Established in 2000, Sarath English Medium School, also known as Sarath Public School, has completed 26 years of academic and administrative excellence in Pedagantyada, Gajuwaka. Families from BJR Nagar Colony, Balacheruvu, Pedagantyada and across Gajuwaka, Visakhapatnam choose Sarath for English-medium education with values. We go beyond traditional textbook education to give students real-world skills, critical thinking and strong ethical values.

Parents ask

Frequently asked

Can't find your answer? Call or WhatsApp the school office on +91 94413 68036.

Is Sarath Public School the same as Sarath English Medium School?

Yes. Sarath English Medium School is also known locally as Sarath Public School. It is one school, at BJR Nagar Colony, Pedagantyada, Gajuwaka, Visakhapatnam 530044.

Where is Sarath English Medium School?

BJR Nagar Colony, Pedagantyada, Gajuwaka, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh 530044. Use Get directions on this page for turn-by-turn navigation.

What are the school timings?

8:30 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Saturday, covering academics, physical activities, club sessions and guided study support.

How do I apply for admission in 2027–28?

Tap Enquire Now, fill in the short form and send it on WhatsApp, or call +91 94413 68036. The office will confirm the class, fees and documents.

What happens in zero hour?

Monday: English speaking, speeches and debates. Tuesday: drawing and crafts. Wednesday: GK questions and riddles. Thursday: role play and life skills. Friday: storytelling and talent show. Saturday: group activities and fun. Theatre Day runs every two weeks.

What facilities does the school have?

Smart classrooms, projector rooms, a front office for parents, and a school ground for cricket and all outdoor games.

What is the Media Club?

A programme for personality development, public speaking, debate and media skills, with early exposure to AI and digital literacy.

How is student safety handled?

The campus has continuous CCTV monitoring, and every child gets 1:1 personalised counselling.
